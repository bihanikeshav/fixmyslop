Your brain craves predictable noise. Silence is too loud; random is distracting. The sweet spot is textured constant sound — the kind that signals the world is moving, it's safe to focus.

Drift builds that texture. Each layer runs independently. Mix rain under a cafe undercurrent. Let the tide pace your breathing. Add forest when the city gets through the glass.

  • Four independent ambient layers, mixed live
  • Tunable session timer — 25, 45, or 60 minutes
  • No music, no lyrics, no algorithm — just the sound you chose
  • Works offline. No account. No ads.
