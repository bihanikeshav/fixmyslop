Marine Biologist — Bioluminescence & Midnight Zone Ecology

I descend to where the sun never reaches — 1,000 metres and deeper — to study the organisms that make their own light. My lab investigates how bioluminescence evolved as a language: luring, warning, hiding, signalling species identity in permanent darkness.
