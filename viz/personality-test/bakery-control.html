Every loaf starts four days before it's baked — flour and water and wild yeast left to talk to each other. We follow the lunar calendar not for romance, but because the wild culture responds. We've seen it with our own eyes.

The lunar
calendar

The moon doesn't hurry, and neither does our starter. Each phase draws a different loaf — open crumb for the bright weeks, tight crumb for the dark ones, crusts that sound like the season.

  • ●
    Dark Rye & Caraway

    Dense, earthy, and long-lasting. A loaf for the start of the cycle — cut thin and layer with anything sharp.

    New moon — 19 Jun
  • ◑
    Country Miche

    The workhorse. 80% white, 20% whole wheat, forty-eight hours in the cold. Open crumb, blistered crust, keeps for four days.

    Waxing — 26 Jun
  • ○
    Seeded Batard

    Sesame, sunflower, poppy. The full moon loaf is generous — the dough's most elastic, the ear opens widest, the crumb sings.

    Full moon — 4 Jul
  • ◐
    Einkorn & Honey

    Ancient wheat, local honey, just a whisper of sweetness. Fragile and beautiful. This one goes fast.

    Waning — 10 Jul
