What you get

Hear yourself honestly.

Most singers never know how they actually sound. PitchPerfect removes the guesswork with instant, visual feedback that makes improvement undeniable.

Live Piano Roll

See your pitch as a continuous curve overlaid on the target melody. Drift, bend, and ornaments rendered frame by frame.

Instant Pitch Detection

Sub-20ms latency using YIN algorithm directly in the browser. No uploads, no server round-trips — just your voice and the app.

Raga & Ornament Detection

PitchPerfect knows Hindustani and Carnatic ragas. It flags gamak, meend, and andolan so you can practise ornaments deliberately.

Session Score & Analytics

Every session ends with a breakdown: pitch accuracy per phrase, worst notes flagged, consistency trend over 30 days.

Streak System

Daily practice streaks, weekly challenges, and raga-specific milestones keep the habit alive without nagging notifications.

XP & Levels

Earn XP for every session, every raga mastered, every streak. Climb from Shishya to Ustadji with 24 levels that mean something.

The process

Three steps to real improvement.

01

Pick a raga or song

Choose from 40 pre-loaded ragas, browse the song library by mood and difficulty, or import any audio file as your target melody.

02

Sing — watch the roll

Grant mic access, start singing. Your pitch traces a live line over the target. Green means on — amber means close — red means adjust now.

03

Review, earn, repeat

After each session see your score, the ornaments you landed, XP earned, and the exact notes to work on. Your level updates immediately.

Progress & Rewards

Progress that feels earned.

Singing every day is hard to sustain without a reason to return. PitchPerfect makes the habit loop tangible — every note you sing moves a number forward.

  • ✦ 24 named levels rooted in Indian classical tradition — from Shishya (student) to Ustadji (grandmaster). Each level has a raga-proficiency requirement, not just an XP gate.
  • ✦ Weekly raga challenges rotate every Monday. Compete on a leaderboard by accuracy, not just time spent — so deliberate practice is rewarded over grinding.
  • ✦ Ornament badges unlock when you consistently land a gamak or meend. Each badge requires 10 correct detections in a row — not just any session.
  • ✦ Streak repair — miss a day once per month and your streak is protected. Because life happens, and shame is not a pedagogy.
