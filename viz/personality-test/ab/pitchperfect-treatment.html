PITCH DETECTION

Your microphone feeds a real-time autocorrelation analyser. Every swara maps to its sargam position: Sa, Re, Ga, Ma, Pa, Dha, Ni — plus komal and tivra variants. Cents deviation shown continuously.

ORNAMENT DETECTION

Gamak (rapid oscillation across a swara), meend (continuous glide), andolan (gentle oscillation on komal notes), and kan (grace note) are recognised from velocity and contour patterns in your pitch curve.
