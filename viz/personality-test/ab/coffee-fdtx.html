Brew

The Craft

How we'd
brew it.

Great coffee is mostly about control — grind, water, time. Pick a method below and follow the steps. We've dialled these in for our roast levels; treat them as a baseline and adjust to taste.

01

Grind fresh, medium-fine

Grind 18g of coffee just before brewing — oxidation begins the moment the cell walls break. Aim for a texture like coarse sea salt, slightly finer than drip. A burr grinder is worth every penny here.

Dose 18 g
Grind Medium-Fine
02

Rinse, pre-heat, bloom

Rinse the filter with 100°C water (removes paper taste, pre-heats the vessel). Discard. Add grounds, then pour 45ml at 93°C in a tight spiral to wet the bed evenly. Wait 45 seconds for the bloom — watch the crust rise as CO₂ escapes.

Bloom water 45 ml
Water temp 93°C
Bloom time 45 sec
03

Pour in two stages

Pour to 200ml total by 1:00, keeping the slurry gently agitated. Then pour to 300ml by 2:00, always spiraling outward from center. Slow, controlled pours mean even extraction. Total draw-down should finish between 3:00–3:30.

Total water 300 ml
Ratio 1 : 16.7
Total time 3:00 – 3:30
04

Taste, adjust, repeat

Sour or thin? Grind finer or add 10g water. Bitter or flat? Grind coarser or try 91°C water. The bed should look flat and even after draw-down — a domed or cracked bed signals uneven flow. Take notes. Great brewing is mostly record-keeping.

If sour Grind finer
If bitter Grind coarser
01

Coarse grind, 30 grams

French press is immersion brewing — it needs a coarse grind so the plunger can do its job without over-extracting. Think rough breadcrumbs. 30g of coffee for a standard 500ml press.

Dose 30 g
Grind Coarse
02

Add 500ml at 92°C, stir once

Pour all the water in one go and stir gently with a wooden spoon to wet all the grounds. Place the lid on but do not plunge yet. Set a timer for 4 minutes.

Water 500 ml
Temp 92°C
03

Skim the crust, plunge slow

At 4 minutes, skim the floating crust off the top — this removes the most bitter fine particles. Then press the plunger down slowly and steadily over 20–30 seconds. Pour immediately; do not let it sit.

Steep time 4 min
Plunge 20–30 sec
01

Dial in the dose: 18g in

Weigh your dose precisely. 18g in the basket is our starting point for a double shot — consistent dosing is the single most important variable to control before touching anything else.

Dose in 18 g
Grind Fine / Espresso
02

Target 36–38g out in 27–32 sec

A 1:2 brew ratio. Start your timer when you engage the pump. A healthy shot should run blonde (not pale white) at the end. If it runs faster than 27 sec, grind finer. Slower than 32 sec, grind coarser.

Yield 36–38 g
Ratio 1 : 2
Time 27–32 sec
03

Taste, adjust one variable at a time

Sour and thin: under-extracted — grind finer. Bitter and harsh: over-extracted — grind coarser, or reduce dose by 0.5g. Change only one thing per shot. After each adjustment, pull, taste, note. Dialling in takes 3–5 shots on a new bag.

Temp 92–94°C
Pressure 9 bar
