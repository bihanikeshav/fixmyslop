About Maya

I am a marine biologist at the Woods Hole Oceanographic Institution, where I lead the Midwater Photobiology Lab. My research addresses a basic question: in a zone without sunlight, how do organisms use light — and what does that light reveal about the evolution of vision, communication, and predation across four kilometres of water column?

Before WHOI, I completed my doctorate at Scripps Institution of Oceanography and a postdoctoral fellowship at the Monterey Bay Aquarium Research Institute. I have participated in 34 deep-sea expeditions, piloting ROVs and deploying custom hyperspectral imaging instruments of my own design.

I also care about making this science legible. The midnight zone is real but invisible to most people — not because it's inaccessible, but because no one has taken them there yet. That's the work of the public talks.
