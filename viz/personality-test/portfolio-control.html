The ocean's deepest layer
holds more undiscovered species
than any habitat on Earth.

I grew up in Lagos, watching fishing boats return before dawn, always wondering what lay beyond the water's surface. That question brought me to Caltech for biochemistry, then to WHOI for a doctorate on deep-sea chemosynthesis, and eventually to the submersible bay of R/V Atlantis.

Today I lead the Midnight Zone Lab at Scripps, where a small team of researchers, engineers, and doctoral students pushes ROV and HOV-based sampling into the hadal zone. We work at the boundary between biology and physics — places where pressure, cold, and darkness force life to solve problems in unexpected ways.
