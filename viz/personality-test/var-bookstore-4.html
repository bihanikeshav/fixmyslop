Staff
Picks

Handwritten notes from the people who shelve them.

Margot — Fiction

Demon Copperhead

Barbara Kingsolver

It destroyed me in the best way. Kingsolver drags Dickens straight into Appalachian opioid country and makes you feel every mile of it. I cried twice and recommended it eleven times.

Sam — Non-fiction

The Serviceberry

Robin Wall Kimmerer

Seventy pages. Carries more weight than most books ten times the length. An ecologist arguing the case for gift economies, with serviceberries as the proof. Leave it on the table after you finish.

Dev — Children’s

The Wilderness

Inga Moore

We stock it in the kids section but half our adults buy it for themselves. The illustrations do something quiet and serious that most picture books are too afraid to attempt. Worth every penny.
