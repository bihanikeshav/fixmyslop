Singing, shakers, scarves, and the parachute — 45 minutes of joyful chaos for little ones and the grown-ups who love them.

What Happens

Every class

  • Hello Circle

    Opening · 5 min

    Names, hellos, and a call-and-response warm-up. Everyone gets a shaker. No one is required to sit still.

  • Songs & Moves

    Core · 20 min

    Three to four nursery rhymes mixed with original songs. Scarves, bells, and toddler-approved choreography (mostly spinning).

  • Parachute Time

    The main event · 10 min

    Everyone grabs an edge. We lift, we wave, we bounce the ball. The moment the whole class exists for.

  • Goodbye Song

    Close · 5 min

    A short wind-down to help toddler bodies know it's time to stop. Mostly works. Always sweet.
