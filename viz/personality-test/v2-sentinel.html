Inside the engine

Three signals. One score. One alert.

Sentinel doesn't alert on individual thresholds for rate, latency, or errors. It correlates all three into a single anomaly score — then surfaces the threshold your team actually wants to act on.

Signal 1

Traffic envelope

Sentinel learns your endpoint-by-endpoint request cadence over 30 days. A spike is only anomalous if it breaks the learned pattern — not a global rate limit.

POST /api/v2/login baseline: 4.2 rps ± 1.1 now: 4.8 rps status: within envelope
Signal 2

Error-rate correlation

A rate spike that correlates with rising 401s or 403s is the fingerprint of a brute-force or credential-stuffing run. Traffic alone is not enough.

401 rate: +340% vs baseline source: 3 CIDR clusters pattern: auth brute-force score: 91 / 100 → ALERT
Signal 3

Canary endpoint guard

Unused endpoints that should never receive real traffic. A single hit is scored at maximum anomaly weight — the quietest signal, the clearest evidence of reconnaissance.

GET /api/internal/ admin/dump expected: 0 req / ever received: 1 req score: 100 / 100 → PAGE
