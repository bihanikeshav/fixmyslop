# Distinctiveness moves (how the best escape sameness)

Reverse-engineered from ~115 genuinely distinctive sites across 15 categories. This
is the positive mirror of the slop manifest: not "what to avoid" but *what the best
actually do*. Use it in **Diverge** and **Ground & commit** — pick the one move that
fits THIS subject and push it everywhere.

**The rule that governs all of it: steal the method, never the tokens.** Copying a
literal token — Stripe's purple gradient, Nike's uppercase, the "Linear look,"
Notion's emoji-doodles — is how slop is born; those get copied into oblivion. What
transfers is the *move* underneath. (See `tensions.md` → "Distinctiveness vs convention.")

## The moves (pick ONE primary, grounded in the subject)
- **Type as identity** — choose/own a typeface distinctive enough to carry the page without a logo, then let it dominate. The single most common move.
- **Own one color, or its absence** — one saturated accent used sparingly, or committed monochrome. Never an evenly-distributed rainbow.
- **Radical subtraction** — strip to near-zero UI; let one element (often full-bleed imagery) be the whole page.
- **A signature image treatment** — one ownable art direction for every photo/illustration (a duotone, a grain, an isometric system, a hand-drawn world).
- **A flexible/generative system** — one rule that yields infinite on-brand variations (a logo or layout that responds).
- **One conceptual gimmick that IS the site** — a single big idea becomes the navigation/experience (best for portfolios & personal sites).
- **A point-of-view voice** — copy with real attitude, carried everywhere. Voice is distinctiveness no one can screenshot-copy.
- **Treat the page like a physical object** — import the sensibility of print, hardware, or a gallery.
- **Editorial restraint in a hype category** — when everyone shouts, the calm, bookish, generous-margin move stands out.
- **Palette/material from the product itself** — derive the system from something real (paint codes, ingredients, the physical thing).
- **A personal metaphor made interactive** — take a concrete object from the subject's actual world and make it a UI element/motif. *This is the core `/personality` move* (cf. the cat lightbulb; pairs with `personality-moves.md`).
- **Name the category default and refuse it** — identify the "sea of sameness" (e.g. fintech blue + Inter + gradient) and deliberately move the opposite way.

## How to use without making NEW slop
- Pick the move that fits the subject's substance; instantiate it from the subject's real world, not from the example brand.
- Commit to ONE radical idea and push it everywhere — distinctiveness is depth on one move, not a pile of effects. The generic middle hedges across many borrowed ones.
- If you can't name the single thing someone will remember, you haven't committed yet.
