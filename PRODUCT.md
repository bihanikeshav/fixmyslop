# Product

## Register

product

## Users

fixmyslop is for AI coding agents, frontend engineers, and design-minded builders who
need to turn a real product brief into an interface without collapsing into the median
AI-generated aesthetic. They work inside repositories and browsers, often under time
pressure, and need both a strong direction and evidence that the implementation remains
usable, responsive, accessible, and faithful to the subject.

## Product Purpose

fixmyslop is an inspectable design instrument. It converts product context and a
subject-specific mechanism into materially different art directions, resolves each into
a coherent implementation system, and verifies the rendered result. Success is not the
absence of known anti-patterns. Success is a working interface whose composition,
interaction, language, and proof could not be noun-swapped onto another brief.

The landing page is the product's demonstration surface, not a separate brand exercise.
It should let a visitor submit a brief, inspect divergent candidates and their evidence,
see why alternatives were rejected, and understand how revision changes the rendered
result.

## Brand Personality

Empirical, audacious, inspectable.

The product speaks with expert confidence but exposes its reasoning. It takes creative
risks in composition and interaction, while making every important decision traceable to
the brief, an observed reference, a deterministic gate, or a visible human judgment.

## Anti-references

- A parody-first anti-slop museum that makes visitors endure bad interfaces before seeing
  the real product.
- Beige or cream editorial “taste,” muted rose accents, reflexive display serifs, and
  specimen-page styling used as a universal badge of refinement.
- Generic AI SaaS: centered headline stacks, purple or fintech-blue accents, floating
  screenshots, glass panels, bento grids, fake metrics, and decorative network diagrams.
- Copies of Impeccable, Taste, Linear, Vercel, Stripe, or a bookmarked reference's skin.
- A deterministic score presented as proof of taste or general superiority.

## Design Principles

1. **Instrument over museum.** The first useful interaction demonstrates the engine; the
   taxonomy and critique material remain secondary reference.
2. **Mechanism before skin.** Model the product's objects, actions, states, and visible
   outcomes before choosing layout, type, color, material, or motion.
3. **Evidence beside the decision.** Show provenance, fit, gates, and rejected options next
   to the candidate they explain.
4. **Diverge in composition and behavior.** Alternative directions must realize the same
   mechanism differently, not merely rotate fonts, palettes, or background treatments.
5. **Truth before promotion.** Preserve claims, expose uncertainty, and bound every
   benchmark statement to the actual evaluated fixtures and conditions.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA is the baseline. Every primary workflow must remain complete with keyboard,
touch, zoom, high-contrast/non-color cues, media failure, and reduced motion. Motion may
explain computation or state change, but it cannot gate content or function. Mobile must
recompose the task rather than shrink the desktop arrangement.
